## Source organization and activation

A **token estimate** is an informational approximation of full active working context: UTF-8 serialized messages/tools projection bytes divided by four, rounded up. The OpenAI formatter projection follows selected-model formatting and configured thinking replay (enabled/disabled plus model-specific format). It is not an exact tokenizer count, actual wire count, upper bound, cumulative usage, remaining capacity or context-window percentage. It makes no extra provider/tokenizer call and does not replace any byte safety budget.

A **Rust module** groups related code; a **library target** is a separate compiled crate within this same package. Explain that distinction before showing declarations. The library is only previously taught **RAG** (retrieval-augmented generation) support. It is not the withdrawn broad support-library extraction, a workspace or a framework.

`lib.rs` exposes:

```rust
pub mod rag;
```
